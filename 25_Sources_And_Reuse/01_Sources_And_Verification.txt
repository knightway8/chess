Sources and verification notes
==============================

The tutorial prose in this collection was written for this library and was not copied from
chess books or channels. General chess concepts were checked against legal move generation
and established rules.

OFFICIAL RULES
https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/e012023
Used to verify board facts, legal concepts, repetition, and move-count draw rules. Rules can
change.

OPENING NAME DATA
https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings
The five source tables are released under the CC0 Public Domain Dedication. The build used
their English names and classification records; move sequences are not distributed here.

EARLY MOVE-TREE COUNTS
https://chessprogramming.org/Perft_Results
The starting-position leaf counts were checked against these published move-generation test
results.

SHANNON ESTIMATE
https://walkofmind.com/programming/chess/Shannon%20-%20Programming%20a%20computer%20for%20playing%20chess.pdf
Claude Shannon’s 1950 paper supplies the assumptions behind the famous game-tree estimate.

ENGINE EVALUATION INTERPRETATION
https://official-stockfish.github.io/docs/stockfish-wiki/Stockfish-FAQ.html
Used only to check the explanation of normalized evaluation and win-draw-loss modeling.
