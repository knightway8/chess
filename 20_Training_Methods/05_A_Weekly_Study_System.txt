A sustainable weekly study system
=================================

Build study around play, review, tactics, endings, and one strategic theme. A balanced week
might include several short tactical sessions, one slow game, a full review, one basic
ending, and one model game. Adjust volume to your time.

Consistency matters more than a heroic session followed by inactivity. Short retrieval
sessions strengthen memory. Schedule difficult calculation when alert and lighter review
when tired.

Let your games choose some topics. If three losses came from king attacks, study defense and
shelter. Keep a smaller portion for broad education so your knowledge continues to expand.

Measure behavior, not only rating. Track blunders from loose pieces, time spent before
critical moves, correct endgame predictions, and opening positions understood. Ratings
contain opponent strength and short-term variance.

Every few weeks, review the plan. Remove exercises that have become mindless, increase
challenge gradually, and preserve rest. Fatigue can train careless habits.

A sample cycle is learn, retrieve, apply, review, and revisit. Read a principle, explain it
from memory, use it in positions, analyze your decisions, then return after a delay. This
converts information into skill.
