Endgame and calculation practice
================================

Combine exact positions with conceptual study. Learn why opposition, checking distance, or a
blockade matters, then verify the mechanism on a legal board. Prose can guide attention but
cannot encode a one-tempo result without exact placement.

Use small position sets repeatedly. Predict win, draw, or loss; calculate the main line;
then test. Track whether the error was move generation, visualization, or evaluation.

Practice calculation at a depth you can hold accurately. Extend only after the imagined
positions remain reliable. Include quiet candidates and defensive resources, not only
forcing attacks.

For rook endings, train activity and checking geometry. For pawn endings, train races and
reserve tempi. For minor-piece endings, train target color, knight routes, and king access.
Rotate themes while revisiting failures.

Tablebases can give perfect results for sufficiently small positions, but the result alone
does not teach a human method. Find the winning or drawing idea and identify moves that
preserve it.

Finish sessions by explaining one position without moving pieces. If the explanation
predicts the verified moves, understanding is becoming portable.
