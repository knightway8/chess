Ruy Lopez
=========

This family is connected to open or semi-open king-pawn play. Development, central contact,
king safety, and tactical timing usually explain more than the label alone. Gambit names
require an explicit account of compensation rather than faith in a trap.

The source contains 200 distinct named labels in this family. Move sequences and
square-coordinate fragments are deliberately omitted. A name identifies a reference point,
not a complete evaluation or repertoire.
