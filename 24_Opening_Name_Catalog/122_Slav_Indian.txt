Slav Indian
===========

This family is connected to delayed central occupation, pressure on a pawn center, or an
asymmetrical queen-pawn structure. Identify which side has space, which breaks challenge it,
and which bishop or knight changes value when the center opens.

The source contains 2 distinct named labels in this family. Move sequences and
square-coordinate fragments are deliberately omitted. A name identifies a reference point,
not a complete evaluation or repertoire.
