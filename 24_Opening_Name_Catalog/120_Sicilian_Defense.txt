Sicilian Defense
================

This family belongs to the asymmetrical Sicilian complex. Use the names below as a map, then
study the exact center, open files, pawn breaks, and king placements on a legal board.
Similar labels can hide important move-order differences.

The source contains 327 distinct named labels in this family. Move sequences and
square-coordinate fragments are deliberately omitted. A name identifies a reference point,
not a complete evaluation or repertoire.
