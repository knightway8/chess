Owen Defense
============

This family is a response to a king-pawn center. Determine whether the center is challenged
immediately or invited forward, then identify the freeing break and the piece most likely to
be restricted. Sound defense needs active counterplay.

The source contains 8 distinct named labels in this family. Move sequences and
square-coordinate fragments are deliberately omitted. A name identifies a reference point,
not a complete evaluation or repertoire.
