Zukertort Opening
=================

This family begins with flank control or flexible development. Its point is normally to
influence the center and choose a favorable structure later. Track transpositions and do not
treat the first move as a complete plan.

The source contains 41 distinct named labels in this family. Move sequences and
square-coordinate fragments are deliberately omitted. A name identifies a reference point,
not a complete evaluation or repertoire.
