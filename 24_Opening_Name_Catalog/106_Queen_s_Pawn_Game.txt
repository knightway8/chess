Queen's Pawn Game
=================

This family belongs to queen-pawn structures or systems. Locate the central tension, the
queen-bishop development problem, and the main freeing break. A familiar setup must still
adapt to the opponent’s structure.

The source contains 49 distinct named labels in this family. Move sequences and
square-coordinate fragments are deliberately omitted. A name identifies a reference point,
not a complete evaluation or repertoire.
