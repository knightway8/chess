Pterodactyl Defense
===================

This is a rare, early-stage, or transpositional opening label. Begin with threats, center,
development, and king safety. Ask what the unusual order permits or delays; do not assume
that rarity proves either strength or weakness.

The source contains 33 distinct named labels in this family. Move sequences and
square-coordinate fragments are deliberately omitted. A name identifies a reference point,
not a complete evaluation or repertoire.
