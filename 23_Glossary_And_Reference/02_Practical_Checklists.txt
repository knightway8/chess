Practical thinking and review checklists
========================================

BEFORE YOUR MOVE

What changed? Is there a check, capture, threat, loose piece, or promotion? What is the
opponent’s strongest idea?

CHOOSING

Name the position’s main need. Generate serious candidates with different purposes. Test
each against the strongest reply. Continue forcing lines until they can be evaluated.

FINAL SAFETY CHECK

Imagine the intended move played. What becomes undefended? Which line opens? What checks,
captures, and threats does the opponent gain?

AFTER THE GAME

Separate the first worse position from the first lost position. Explain the error type and
choose one training response.
